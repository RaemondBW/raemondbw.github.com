How every San Francisco precinct shifted on Sunset Dunes

In 2024, voters closed the Upper Great Highway to cars and made it a park (Proposition K). This year Proposition G asked whether to let cars back on weekdays.

Shift in support for the park, by precinct

Each arrow shows how much a precinct’s vote moved between Yes on K in 2024 and No on G in 2026, both of which kept the road car-free.

Notes: Support for the park is Yes on Proposition K (Nov. 5, 2024) and No on Proposition G (Nov. 3, 2026), as a share of Yes + No votes. 2024 figures are the certified final Statement of the Vote. 2026 figures are from and only include ballots counted so far, so they will change as late-arriving mail ballots are tabulated. Both elections use the precinct lines drawn in 2022. Precincts with fewer than votes in either election are not drawn. Very small shifts are drawn with a short minimum length so their direction stays visible.

Sources: San Francisco Department of Elections; precinct boundaries from DataSF.
